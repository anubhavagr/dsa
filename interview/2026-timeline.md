# 📆 2026 Application Timeline — turning preparation into an offer

**Goal stated:** "crack Google L5 in 2026." Work backwards from that.

Google's loop: referral/app → recruiter screen (~1 wk) → phone screen or direct onsite (~1–3 wks to schedule) → onsite (4–5 rounds: 2–3 coding, 1 system design, 1 Googleyness & leadership) → hiring committee → team match → offer. **End to end: 4–8 weeks.** So an offer *landing* in 2026 requires the onsite in December — which means applying by early-to-mid November. If the offer slips to January, you still *cracked it in 2026's window* — the process started, the onsite happened, the bar was cleared. Plan for both.

## Week-by-week, aligned to the study calendar

| When | Study calendar | Application track (parallel, ~2 h/week) |
|---|---|---|
| **Sep 28 – Oct 11** (W1–2) | Foundations | Write the resume NOW while energy is high: impact bullets (X improved Y by Z%), the repo itself is a story. Start a referrals list: ex-colleagues, alumni, LinkedIn 2nd-degree Googlers |
| **Oct 12 – Nov 1** (W3–5) | Lists, stacks, trees | Draft 8 STAR stories → [googleyness-star.md](googleyness-star.md). Resume v2 review by one trusted senior |
| **Nov 2 – Nov 15** (W6–7) | Heaps, graphs | **Apply window opens:** referral requests first (aim for 2–3 Googlers), then careers portal. Also apply 2–3 comparable companies (Meta, Uber, LinkedIn) for live-fire practice and leverage |
| **Nov 16 – Nov 29** (W8–9) | DP deep dive | Recruiter calls. Say the target: "L5 senior" — your YOE invites L4, your interview performance decides. Ask which phone-screen window works with your job |
| **Nov 30 – Dec 13** (W10–11) | Company-tagged sets | **Phone screen (target: Dec 8–15)** — it's literally Week 11's material. Reschedule fearlessly if W11's Phase-3 gate failed; a strong later screen beats a rushed failed one |
| **Dec 14 – Dec 20** (W12) | Interview sims | Onsites scheduled for **Dec 21 – Jan 9**. Week 12 is your dress rehearsal; protect it |
| **Dec 21 – Jan 9** (W13 +) | Final review → L5-SET | **Onsite season.** 45–60 min/day maintenance: redo 🔁 queue + L5-SET rows; one system-design prompt every 2 days; stories rehearsed aloud |
| **Jan** | L5-SET 3–4/week | Team match / HC / negotiate. Keep practicing until the offer is signed — momentum dies in team-match gaps |

## Rules that protect the plan

1. **Applications never eat study days.** The 2 h/week application budget comes out of evenings you'd otherwise waste, not out of the daily loop.
2. **Phone screen only after Gate 4 passes** (two unseen mediums, 35 min each, optimal, narrated — see [L5-BAR.md](../L5-BAR.md)). Booking it before you're ready converts your best referral into a cooling period.
3. **Practice companies first.** Do Meta/Uber/LinkedIn phone screens before Google's if the timing allows — real adrenaline is unreproducible.
4. **Every real interview becomes study material:** write down every question within an hour, add misses to the 🔁 queue.
5. **A failed Google loop = 90-day cooldown.** That's the cost of skipping gate checks. The plan exists so you never pay it.
6. If the process stretches into January: **do not stop** the maintenance cadence between phone screen and onsite — that gap is where offers die.
