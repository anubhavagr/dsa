# 🗣️ Googleyness & Leadership — 8 STAR stories (build during October)

Google dedicates a full onsite round (Googleyness & Leadership, G&L) to behavior, and for L5 the bar is "senior scope": influence beyond your team, handling ambiguity, developing others. With 3.5 YOE your stories must punch ABOVE your tenure — depth of ownership substitutes for years.

**Schedule:** draft 2 stories/week during Weeks 3–6 (30 min each, alongside the daily loop). Rehearse aloud from Oct 31; polish after every real interview.

## The 8 canvases (fill one per story)

```
## Story N — <one-word theme>
Situation (2 sentences, scale numbers): 
Task (what YOU owned — "I", never "we"): 
Action (3–4 steps, incl. one decision you made against pushback): 
Result (number + what changed after): 
L5 escalation (the follow-up they WILL ask): 
  - "What would you do differently?" → 
  - "How did the other people feel?" → 
  - "What if you had 10x the scale?" → 
```

## Required coverage (map your experiences onto these)

1. **Leadership without authority** — drove something to done when nobody reported to you. (The classic L5 probe: "leadership beyond title".)
2. **Ambiguity** — took a fuzzy problem and shaped the plan. L5 signal: you WROTE the plan others followed.
3. **Conflict / disagreement** — technical or cross-team, where you were challenged. Must end with how the relationship survived.
4. **Failure you owned** — real one, with your name on the mistake and the systemic fix after. (Not a humble-brag. Google smells those.)
5. **Impact at scale** — biggest thing you shipped; numbers or it didn't happen.
6. **Cross-functional work** — PM/design/data/other-team collaboration where you translated between worlds.
7. **Developing others / mentorship** — onboarding, mentoring, leveling someone up. Thin at 3.5 YOE is fine — a junior-dev story told with depth beats an invented lead story.
8. **Bias for action** — shipped the small thing rather than debating the big thing; or killed your own project early.

## Google-specific G&L probes to pre-answer

- "Tell me about a time you strongly disagreed with your manager" (they push: "and if they still said no?")
- "How do you decide what to work on when everything is on fire?"
- "Something you did that was Googley" (they literally use the word — helpfulness/candor beyond your job)
- "A time you worked on something nobody asked you to" (→ this DSA repo + the application timeline is a genuine answer — use it)

## Rehearsal discipline

- Each story: ≤ 3 minutes spoken. Practice with a timer; interviewers cut you off mid-story and good stories survive the cut.
- Every story ends in a NUMBER (latency saved, revenue, hours/week, users). No number? Estimate honestly and say it's an estimate.
- Record yourself once per story on Oct 31 / Nov 14 — watching yourself is the fastest fix for rambling.
- Bring the stories into December sims: after each Week-12 simulation, tell one story aloud as if the debrief were a G&L round.
